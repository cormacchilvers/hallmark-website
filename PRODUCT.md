# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small and medium business owners who are ambitious and frustrated. They know their current website undersells them. They arrive at Hallmark Studio already convinced they need better, and they are looking for a studio that matches their own standards without needing to be impressed by performance theatre. They are decisive, image-conscious, and short on time.

## Product Purpose

Hallmark Studio is a premium web design agency established in 2026. The website exists to demonstrate that the studio is the standard, not just a vendor. Success means a prospective client reads three lines and thinks "these are my people" before they have seen a single portfolio piece. Secondary success is a qualified inquiry through the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, Ambitious, Refined. The studio is new, unapologetic about quality, and does not need to borrow credibility from anyone. The voice is confident without arrogance, authoritative without stuffiness. Every word earns its place.

## Anti-references

- **Over-animated agency sites**: cursor blobs, excessive parallax, 3D WebGL for its own sake. Motion should serve communication, not perform effort.
- **Generic SaaS templates**: hero plus three-feature grid plus pricing table. The Webflow marketplace aesthetic. Horizontal sameness.
- **Dark neon / dev-tool aesthetics**: glowing purple or green on near-black. Feels like a build tool, not a design studio.
- **Stock-photo-heavy sites**: smiling team photos, iStock office imagery, generic "laptop on desk" filler. Every image must earn its presence.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof.** Hallmark Studio builds websites for a living. The website must make that claim without stating it. No self-congratulation. Show, never describe.
2. **Restraint is the luxury signal.** Premium brands remove, they do not add. White space is not emptiness. Every element that does not earn its place is cut.
3. **Confidence over enthusiasm.** No exclamation marks in headlines. No "we're passionate about..." copy. Authority is quiet.
4. **Type carries the brand.** The editorial serif is not decoration. It is the primary identity vehicle. Let it do the work.
5. **First impression is the only impression.** The studio name is Hallmark Studio. A hallmark is a guarantee of quality. Every interaction must justify that name.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Adequate color contrast on all text, keyboard-navigable interactive elements, screen-reader-friendly semantic HTML. Reduced-motion media query respected for all GSAP animations.
