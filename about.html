Who we are

Hallmark Studio was built by two people in Chelmsford, Essex, who believed small businesses deserved better than templates and broken promises.

We started this because we kept seeing the same thing — great businesses with terrible websites, losing customers to competitors who simply showed up better online. That felt like a problem worth solving.

We're a small team by design. Every website we build is handled directly by us — no outsourcing, no account managers, no handoffs. When you work with Hallmark Studio, you work with the people who actually care about the outcome.
