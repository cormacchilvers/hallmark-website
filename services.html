Common Questions

What's included in the website build?

You get a fully custom 8-page website designed and built around your brand — no templates, no page builders. Includes mobile-first responsive design, on-page SEO setup, Google Business Profile configuration, and a 50% deposit / 50% on launch payment structure.

Can I cancel my SEO plan?

All monthly plans have a 6-month minimum term — SEO needs time to work, and we wouldn't be doing right by you if we let you cancel before results have had a chance to compound. After the initial 6 months, you're on a rolling monthly contract and can cancel with 30 days' notice.

What is the 60-day walk-away period?

If you're on a monthly plan and it's not working in the first 60 days, you can walk away with no cancellation fee and no questions asked. We think that's a fair way to start a working relationship — you shouldn't be locked in before you've had a chance to see what we do.

Do you work with businesses outside Essex?

Yes. While we're based in Chelmsford and know the Essex market well, we work with businesses across the UK. Our website builds are fully remote-friendly — everything is handled via video calls, email, and our project management process.

What is reputation management and do I need it?

Reputation management is the practice of actively building and protecting your business's online image — primarily through reviews on Google and other platforms. If you're in a service industry where customers check reviews before buying, it matters. It's included as standard in our Pro plan.
