You've been quoted £300. You've been quoted £8,000. Both for "a website."

It's confusing, and it often feels deliberate. Let's cut through it.

## Why prices are all over the place

Website prices vary this much because "a website" is a completely meaningless term. It covers everything from a five-minute Wix account with your name and a phone number to a fully custom-built site with professional copywriting, SEO foundations, conversion strategy, and an ongoing maintenance plan.

Most quotes you'll get don't explain what's included. They also don't tell you what it'll cost to keep the thing running, which is where a lot of business owners get a nasty surprise six months in.

## The DIY route: Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy

These platforms let you build something yourself for £10–£20 per month. On the surface, that sounds like a deal.

The reality: it takes longer than you think, the templates all look the same, and unless you know what you're doing with SEO, your site won't show up on Google, which means you've spent 20 hours building something nobody finds.

There's also the time cost. Most small business owners spend 15–25 hours building a DIY website for the first time. Work out your hourly rate and see whether that's actually cheap.

That said: if the alternative is no website at all, a DIY site is a starting point. Just don't confuse having a website with having a website that works.

## What £500–£800 gets you from a freelancer

There are plenty of freelance web designers in Essex at this price point. Some are excellent. Some use templates and call them custom.

At this range, you'll often get a site that looks reasonable and works on mobile. What you frequently won't get: serious SEO setup, fast page speed, a clear conversion strategy, or much support after it goes live.

Before you hire any freelancer, ask: "How will this site get found on Google?" If they can't answer clearly and specifically, you know what you're getting.

## What £1,000–£2,500 gets you from a small studio

This is where you start getting something built to perform, not just to exist.

At this price, from a studio that knows what it's doing, you should expect:

- Custom design (not a template with your logo dropped in)
- Mobile-first, fast-loading pages
- Proper SEO setup: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, schema markup
- Google Search Console and Analytics connected
- Copywriting included, so it actually sounds like a person, not a brochure
- Clear calls to action designed to get you enquiries

This is where our one-off website builds sit, from £999. Not the cheapest option available. But an option built to get you a return.

## What the monthly plans are about

Many agencies, including us, offer monthly plans that combine the website with ongoing SEO management.

Why pay monthly instead of buying outright? A few honest reasons.

**The SEO work is ongoing.** Getting onto page one of Google isn't a task you do once and tick off. It requires content, technical maintenance, review building, and local signals, built up consistently over months. A monthly plan keeps that work happening.

**You spread the cost.** A £999 upfront build isn't possible for every business at every moment. At £199/month, you get a professional site and monthly SEO management for roughly the price of a couple of small jobs.

Our plans start at £199/month. We ask for a 6-month minimum, because that's genuinely how long it takes to see SEO results. Anyone who says differently is either misleading you or selling something that isn't really SEO. After 6 months, it's rolling monthly.

## What to ask any web designer before you hire them

1. Can you show me examples of sites you've built for similar businesses?
2. How will this site get found on Google, specifically?
3. What does it cost after launch (hosting, updates, support)?
4. Who owns the website, me or you?
5. What happens if I want to leave or move elsewhere?

If you get vague answers to any of those, keep looking.
