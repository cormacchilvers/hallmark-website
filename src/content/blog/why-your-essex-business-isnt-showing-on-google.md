You search your own business on Google. Nothing. Or worse, a competitor's name comes up where yours should be.

It happens more often than you'd think. And every day it happens, someone in Chelmsford, Colchester, or Brentwood is looking for exactly what you do, and finding someone else.

Here's why it happens, and the one thing you can do about it today.

## The most common reason: your Google Business Profile doesn't exist

Google Business Profile (the listing that shows on Google Maps and in local results) is free. It shows your business name, address, phone number, opening hours, and reviews when someone searches for a service near them.

If you haven't claimed it, you're invisible.

It doesn't matter how long you've been trading. It doesn't matter whether you have a website. If your Google Business Profile isn't claimed and verified, you won't appear in the local map results, those three listings at the very top of Google that show up when someone searches "plumber Chelmsford" or "hair salon Southend."

Those three get the calls. Everything below is fighting for scraps.

## The second most common reason: your listing is incomplete

A lot of business owners claimed their profile years ago and never touched it again. The phone number is wrong. The opening hours say Monday to Friday when you actually open weekends. The description is blank.

Google rewards profiles that are complete and active. A sparse, unchanged listing tells Google, and potential customers, that you're not serious about being found.

## The third reason: no reviews

This one's harder to fix overnight, but it matters enormously. Google uses the number and recency of reviews as a signal of trust. Two businesses with identical websites, the one with 20 recent reviews will usually outrank the one with none.

You can't buy genuine reviews, and you shouldn't try. But you can make it dead simple for happy customers to leave one. (More on that in another post.)

## The 1-hour fix: claim and complete your profile

Here's what to do today.

1. **Search your business name on Google.** If you see "Claim this business" or no listing at all, you need to act.
2. **Go to business.google.com.** Sign in with a Google account and follow the steps to claim your listing.
3. **Verify your business.** Google usually sends a postcard with a code to your address. It takes a few days to arrive, start now.
4. **Fill everything in.** Name, address, phone, website, opening hours, description, photos. Don't leave anything blank.
5. **Add real photos.** Your premises, your team, examples of your work. Phone photos are absolutely fine. Google actively favours profiles with images.

That's it. For most Essex small businesses, completing their Google Business Profile is the single highest-return thing they can do online, and it costs nothing but an hour of time.

## Why your website still matters

Your Google Business Profile gets you into the map results. But when someone clicks through to your website, that's where the decision gets made.

A slow website. No phone number on the page. Text that's unreadable on a phone. An unclear answer to "what do you actually do and where?" That's where people leave, and where you lose them to the business below you that does have those things sorted.

Getting found and turning visitors into customers are two separate problems. Both cost you if they're not fixed.
